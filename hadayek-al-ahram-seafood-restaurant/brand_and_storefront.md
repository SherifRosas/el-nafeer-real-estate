# Branding and Storefront Design: Hadayek Al-Ahram Seafood Restaurant
**Date:** August 2026  
**Location:** Al-Awadi Street, Opposite Al-Hadaeq Club, Hadayek Al-Ahram, Giza  
**Storefront Profile:** 150 m² Corner Unit with 4 Roller-Shutter Bays (Angled Corner)

---

## 1. Search Engine Optimized Name Suggestions

With a larger 150 m² footprint and high-end dine-in capacity, the name should sound established and trustworthy, while retaining strong local SEO keywords.

### Option A: The Local Authority (Recommended)
*   **Arabic:** الجمل للمأكولات البحرية - نادي الحدائق (El-Gamel Seafood - Nadi Al-Hadaeq)
*   **English:** Hadayek Al-Ahram Seafood & Dine-In
*   **SEO Benefit:** Directly captures the most searched terms in Giza ("الجمل للمأكولات البحرية") and links it to the landmark club opposite the shop.

### Option B: The Specialty Seafood House
*   **Arabic:** صياد الهضبة - مأكولات بحرية (Sayyad Al-Hadaba - Seafood House)
*   **English:** Al-Hadaba Seafood House
*   **SEO Benefit:** Targets the local nickname for Pyramids Gardens ("Al-Hadaba"), building immediate local community trust.

## 2. Storefront Exterior Design Guidelines (Based on New Site Photo)

The uploaded photo shows a prominent, wide corner shopfront with an angled corner column (featuring the builder's marble plaque) and 4 roller-shutter openings (2 on the left street face, 2 on the right street face). 

We recommend a continuous, high-visibility layout:

```
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  [Shutter 1]        [Shutter 2]          [Corner Column]   [Shutter 3]        [Shutter 4]
 ┌───────────┐      ┌───────────┐      ┌─────────────────┐ ┌───────────┐      ┌───────────┐

```

### Signage & Fascia (The Visual Magnet)
*   **Continuous L-Shaped Cladding:** Mount a matte navy-blue aluminum composite (Alucobond) panel system directly onto the thick concrete lintel beam running above all 4 shutters. This runs continuously around the corner.
*   **Backlit 3D Lettering:**
    *   **Left Sign:** *Restaurant Name* in elegant white acrylic letters backlit with warm LEDs.
    *   **Corner Hub:** Mount the circular brand emblem (coral shrimp & navy fish outline) directly above the angled column.
    *   **Right Sign:** *Restaurant Name* + **"صالة طعام وتوصيل"** (Dine-in & Delivery).
*   **Awnings:** Install retractable navy-blue canvas awnings over the shutter windows to block direct sun (to protect the fresh display and cool the dine-in windows).

### Shutter Configuration (4 Openings)
1.  **Shutter 1 (Far Left - Dine-In Window):** Install a large sheet of clear glass. Inside, position a wooden high-top bar counter with stools. Passersby will see customers dining, which naturally attracts more walk-in traffic.
2.  **Shutter 2 (Left-Center - Main Entrance):** Central double glass swing doors. This serves as the main entrance for dine-in guests and families.
3.  **Shutter 3 (Right-Center - Fresh Seafood Display):** The visual centerpiece. A sealed double-glazed glass display showcasing fresh fish and prawns lying on a bed of crushed ice under clean white spotlights. Customers pick their fish here before entering.
4.  **Shutter 4 (Far Right - Takeaway & Delivery Hatch):** A sliding glass window. Talabat and elmenus captains collect their orders here directly from the kitchen packing zone, keeping delivery drivers outside the main dining lobby.

## 3. Interior Zoning & Layout Plan (150 m²)

The 150 m² floor plan allows for a logical separation of zones to ensure smooth operations, health code compliance, and excellent ventilation:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      BACK OFFICE                       │
├───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┤
│                           │                            │
│    WASHING & CLEANING     │    HOT KITCHEN ZONE        │
│    ZONE (~20 m²)          │    (~60 m²)                │
│    - Scaling & gutting    │    - Grills & Fryers       │
│    - Smell isolation      │    - Hood & Roof Chimney   │
│                           │                            │
├───────────────────────────┴──────────────┬─────────────┤
│                                          │             │
│    DINE-IN SEATING AREA                  │  CASHIER &  │
│    (~40 m²)                              │  DISPLAY    │
│    - 8 Tables (24-32 Seats)              │  (~30 m²)   │
│                                          │             │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┴─────────────┘
                ▲                                ▲
          [Main Entrance]                [Display Window]
```

*   **Hot Kitchen Zone (~60 m²):** Located at the back-right, directly adjacent to Shutter 4 (delivery hatch). Equipped with a large flat-top grill, commercial deep fryers, and a singary baking oven. It requires a dedicated exhaust hood with ducting routed up to the building’s roof.
*   **Washing & Cleaning Zone (~20 m²):** A closed, tiled room at the back-left where fresh fish is scaled, gutted, and prepared. This room must have high-volume drain outlets and a separate exhaust fan to completely isolate raw fish odors from the dining area.
*   **Dine-In Seating Area (~40 m²):** Positioned on the left side (behind Shutters 1 and 2). It accommodates 8 tables (24 to 32 seats). Warm lighting, wood-grain tables, and comfortable seating are used to establish a pleasant environment.
*   **Fresh Display & Cashier Lobby (~30 m²):** Behind Shutter 3. Features the ice display counter and digital scales. The cashier register is placed here so customers order by weight, pay, and are then directed to their dining table or given a buzzer for takeaway.
