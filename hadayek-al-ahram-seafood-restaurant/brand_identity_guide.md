## 1. Color Palette / لوحة الألوان

Our selected color palette is designed to contrast elegantly against the warm sandstone/beige brick facade of the building (shown in the storefront photo), ensuring a premium, sophisticated culinary aesthetic that avoids cheap fast-food neon.

```
┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐
│  PRIMARY DARK   │ │ PRIMARY ACCENT  │ │ SECONDARY LIGHT │
│     #0B2840     │ │     #C89D52     │ │     #F4EFE3     │
└─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘
┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐
│  SEAFOOD GREEN  │ │    TEXT DARK    │ │    ALERT/CTA    │
│     #3A6351     │ │     #232529     │ │     #C0503E     │
└─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘
```

### 1. Primary Dark: Midnight Ocean Depth
*   **Hex:** `#0B2840`
*   **CMYK:** `C=83%, M=37%, Y=0%, K=75%`
*   **RGB:** `R=11, G=40, B=64`
*   **Justification:** Provides high-contrast readability and premium maritime weight against the warm, reflective sandstone building facade, ensuring visibility from across the busy club street.

### 2. Primary Accent: Burnished Gold
*   **Hex:** `#C89D52`
*   **CMYK:** `C=0%, M=21%, Y=59%, K=22%`
*   **RGB:** `R=200, G=157, B=82`
*   **Justification:** Harmonizes with the natural warm tones of the sandstone masonry while adding a touch of premium craftsmanship and elegance to signage borders, logo accents, and typography.

### 3. Secondary Light: Sandstone Cream
*   **Hex:** `#F4EFE3`
*   **CMYK:** `C=0%, M=2%, Y=7%, K=4%`
*   **RGB:** `R=244, G=239, B=227`
*   **Justification:** Replaces stark, sterile white with a warm sandstone tone that visually integrates menu layouts, interior walls, and digital backgrounds with the physical building facade.

### 4. Seafood Green: Deep Sea Sage
*   **Hex:** `#3A6351`
*   **CMYK:** `C=41%, M=0%, Y=18%, K=61%`
*   **RGB:** `R=58, G=99, B=81`
*   **Justification:** Connects the brand with marine freshness and organic health without the cheapness of neon green, serving as a marker for salads, fresh catches, and green accents.

### 5. Text Dark: Charcoal Ink
*   **Hex:** `#232529`
*   **CMYK:** `C=15%, M=10%, Y=0%, K=84%`
*   **RGB:** `R=35, G=37, B=41`
*   **Justification:** Provides optimal text legibility for menus, receipts, and user interfaces while avoiding the harshness of pure black, maintaining a warm, organic feel.

### 6. Alert/CTA: Seafood Terracotta
*   **Hex:** `#C0503E`
*   **CMYK:** `C=0%, M=58%, Y=68%, K=25%`
*   **RGB:** `R=192, G=80, B=62`
*   **Justification:** Serves as an appetite-inducing call-to-action color for delivery buttons and hot deal tags, representing cooked shrimp/crustaceans without using alarming, fast-food red.

## 2. Typography System / نظام الخطوط والكتابة

A bilingual typography system that balances high legibility on high-speed streets with elevated restaurant branding:

### Arabic Typography (Right-to-Left)
*   **Heading/Logo Font:** **Cairo (Bold/Black)**
    *   *Type:* Google Fonts (Modern Geometric Kufic)
    *   *Rationale:* A heavy, structured Kufic script. It possesses high visibility from 20 meters away, making it ideal for the storefront lintel, while remaining extremely clean when scaled down to mobile screen headers.
*   **Body Font:** **Tajawal (Regular/Medium)**
    *   *Type:* Google Fonts (Modern Arabic Sans-Serif)
    *   *Rationale:* Highly legible at small sizes (12-16px). Its open counters ensure readability on low-contrast screens and thermal receipt prints.

### English Typography (Left-to-Right)
*   **Heading/Logo Font:** **Cormorant Garamond (Bold)**
    *   *Type:* Google Fonts (Classic Premium Serif)
    *   *Rationale:* An elegant serif typeface that evokes the premium, heritage-focused atmosphere of a Mediterranean seafood bistro.
*   **Body Font:** **Poppins (Regular/Medium)**
    *   *Type:* Google Fonts (Geometric Sans-Serif)
    *   *Rationale:* A friendly, geometric sans-serif that pairs naturally with the structure of Tajawal on bilingual menus.

### Font Pairing Rationale
The Kufic geometric lines of **Cairo** reflect the structured proportions of the geometric sans-serif body fonts (**Tajawal** and **Poppins**), while the classical elegance of **Cormorant Garamond** introduces a premium contrast, establishing a balanced blend of modern structure and culinary heritage.

## 4. Signage Application Guide / دليل تركيب اللوحات الخارجية

Based on the photo of the ground-floor corner unit under the residential building, the signage is planned to maximize visibility from Al-Awadi street and the club opposite the shop:

![Storefront Signage Mockup](/C:/Users/Sherif-Rosas/.gemini/antigravity/brain/a57146e1-d949-4375-bab9-626128bbae33/render_2_1_storefront_both_sides_1787528179947.jpg)

### 1. Main L-Shaped Sign (اللوحة الرئيسية)
*   **Location:** Mounted directly onto the front and side face of the concrete lintel beam.
*   **Material:** Matte Midnight Navy (`#0B2840`) aluminum composite cladding (Alucobond) to cover the rough concrete beams.
*   **Lettering:** Individual 3D acrylic letters. The Arabic name **"الجمل للمأكولات البحرية"** in bold Cairo font, and the English name **"El-Gamel Seafood"** below it in Cormorant Garamond.
*   **Illumination:** Warm white (3000K) halo backlighting behind each letter. This casts a soft, premium glow against the navy cladding, ensuring readability without the glare of cheap front-lit plastic boxes.

### 2. Side Sign (لوحة الخدمات الجانبية)
*   **Location:** The side face of the L-beam.
*   **Content:** **"صالة طعام - تيك أواي - ديليفري"** / **"Dine-in - Takeaway - Delivery"** in backlit Gold acrylic lettering.
*   **Purpose:** Directs delivery drivers and passing cars to the right-side pickup window.

### 3. Corner Pillar Emblem (أيقونة الركن)
*   **Location:** Placed at the corner junction of the L-beam, directly above the column.
*   **Sign type:** A 1-meter round, double-sided projecting light box featuring **Concept C ("The Club Landmark")** with "الجمل / El-Gamel" in Navy and Gold.
*   **Illumination:** Steady 3000K warm white internal LED lighting. Acts as a prominent beacon for pedestrians walking along both streets.

### 4. Shutter and Glass Graphics (رسومات النوافذ)
*   **Bay 1 (Dine-in Window):** Left shutter. Installs clear double-glazing with a thin, sandblasted gold border.
*   **Bay 2 (Entrance):** Main entrance doors. Bilingual welcome vinyl greeting: *"أهلاً بكم في صالة الجمل للمأكولات البحرية | Welcome to El-Gamel Seafood"* in gold lettering.
*   **Bay 3 (Display Window):** Fresh display shutter. A gold vinyl graphic at the bottom of the glass reading *"العرض الطازج اليومي | Daily Fresh Catch"*.
*   **Bay 4 (Delivery Hatch):** Far right shutter. An opaque, navy vinyl wrap featuring a clean wave line pattern (Concept B icon) to hide the busy kitchen packing area, leaving a sliding clear glass hatch for order handover.

## 6. Brand Voice & Do/Don't Rules / قواعد لغة العلامة التجارية

### DO / افعل:
*   **Use Warm, Local Hospitality:** Frame all greetings around family gathering and freshness (e.g., *"من البحر إلى طاولتكم" / "From the sea to your table"*).
*   **Prioritize Arabic:** Arabic text must be placed on top or to the right and be **15-20% larger** than English text in all bilingual layouts to reflect the local demographic.
*   **Keep Gold Accents Restrained:** Use gold only for fine lines, emblems, and signage borders. It should convey quality, not gaudiness.
*   **Focus on Local Fish Photography:** Always use high-quality, steamy photography of local Egyptian favorites (Grilled Tilapia with bran, Singary Mullet, baked Shrimp clay pots).

### DON'T / لا تفعل:
*   **No Western Seafood Clichés:** Never use imagery of lobsters, king crabs, or salmon. These are not served at this restaurant and confuse customers.
*   **No Cartoon Graphics:** Never use bubbly text, cute cartoon fish, or blue ocean waves. The restaurant is an upscale family landmark, not a children's shop.
*   **No Pure Black (#000000) or Pure Red:** Avoid sterile black/red. Use **Charcoal Ink (`#232529`)** and **Seafood Terracotta (`#C0503E`)** to keep the visual tone organic and warm.
*   **No Gradients on Signage:** Signage and menu backgrounds must be flat, solid brand colors (**Midnight Navy** or **Sandstone Cream**). Gradients fade under Egypt's intense sun and look cheap.
