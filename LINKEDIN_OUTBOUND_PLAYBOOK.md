## 🎯 Step 3: Closing the Lead (إغلاق الصفقة)
When a lead replies with interest, do not explain everything in text. Immediately guide them to a call:

1. **If they ask about price:**
   > *"Our pricing is fully customized based on the number of sales agents, numbers, and the CRM integration requirements. Let's hop on a quick 10-minute Zoom call to understand your setup, and I will give you a tailored proposal."*
2. **If they ask about features/integrations:**
   > *"Yes, we support complete API integration with your existing CRM/systems. Let's schedule a brief call so my engineering team can assess your current system and show you how it integrates."*
