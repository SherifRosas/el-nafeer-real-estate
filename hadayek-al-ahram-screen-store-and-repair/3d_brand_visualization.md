# 3D Brand Book & Storefront Visualization / دليل الهوية البصرية المجسمة
**Brand Mark / الشعار الرسمي:** An abstract icon of a glowing television screen displaying two shaking hands, forming an "E" shape (representing El-Ekhwa - Brotherhood).  
**Color Palette / الألوان:** Matte Charcoal Black (`#121212`), Neon Cyan (`#00F0FF`), Ice White (`#F5F5F7`).

---

## Part 1: Exterior Signage & Facade Visualization / لوحات المحل والواجهة

### Asset 1.1: Storefront Night View (Key Visual) / واجهة المحل ليلًا
An ultra-detailed photorealistic nighttime exterior 3D architectural render of the "El-Ekhwa Screens & Repair" shop in Hadayek Al-Ahram. It showcases the high-contrast neon-cyan signage standing out against the bright warm-white spillover from the neighboring finishes shop.

*   **Midjourney / DALL-E 3 Prompt:**
    ```text
    A photorealistic 3D architectural nighttime exterior visualization of a modern electronics and screen repair shop on the ground floor of a Giza sandstone apartment building in Giza, Egypt. The shop has a main facade. MAIN SIGN: A horizontal metal signboard cladded in matte charcoal black paneling. Mounted on this is the logo (a minimalist TV screen outline forming an "E" shape) and the Arabic text "الأخوة للشاشات والصيانة" in 3D raised white acrylic lettering backlit with vibrant neon-cyan LED halo lighting. Running along the very edge of the signboard frame is a thin, continuous cool-white LED strip light, giving the illusion of a borderless television screen floating on the building's facade. LEFT WALL: A vertical smooth concrete column painted in dark charcoal grey, featuring a large high-contrast QR code poster with Arabic text "امسح الرمز" and a dummy 55-inch thin-bezel LED TV mounted behind a clear protective glass sheet. THE CONTRAST: Directly to the right, the neighboring shop has extremely bright, warm golden-yellow lighting from hanging chandeliers and light fixtures, creating a dramatic contrast between the cool tech-cyan glow of our shop and the neighbor's yellow glare. 8K, architectural lighting, highly detailed.
    ```
