# Feasibility Study: Screen Sales & Repair Shop / دراسة الجدوى: محل بيع وصيانة الشاشات
**Project / المشروع:** El-Ekhwa Screens & Repair / الأخوة للشاشات والصيانة  
**Location / الموقع:** Hadayek Al-Ahram, Giza / حدائق الأهرام، الجيزة  
**Date / التاريخ:** August 2026 / أغسطس 2026  

---

## 1. Executive Summary / الملخص التنفيذي

This study evaluates the financial viability of upgrading "El-Ekhwa" shop in Hadayek Al-Ahram. By combining a low-cost physical storefront facelift (budget of ~10,000 EGP) with a digital customer portal (chatbot, online catalog, QR-based repair tracking, and automated accounting), we can significantly increase customer acquisition and service speed with minimal capital risk.

### Strategic Advantages / المزايا الاستراتيجية:
1.  **High-Margin Core Business:** Screen repair (especially backlight and power board repair) has low material costs and high labor value, yielding margins over 60%.
2.  **Bridging Physical and Digital:** The physical QR code on the shop facade routes walk-by traffic directly to the web catalog and AI chatbot. This converts after-hours traffic into repair bookings.
3.  **Low CapEx:** Since the shop is already leased and operating, the additional capital required to execute this upgrade is minimal, leading to a fast payback period.

## 4. Sourcing & Profit Margin Analysis / تحليل مصادر التوريد وهوامش الأرباح

The business has two main revenue streams: **Screen Repair (Service-based)** and **Refurbished/New Screen Sales (Product-based)**.

### A. Screen Repair Margins / هوامش أرباح الصيانة
Screen repairs are highly lucrative because the cost of parts is low compared to the cost of purchasing a new TV.

1.  **LED Backlight Strip Replacement (Most Common TV Issue):**
    *   *Average Raw Material Cost:* 300 - 500 EGP (Wholesale from Bab Al-Louq or El-Ataba).
    *   *Retail Repair Charge:* 1,500 - 2,500 EGP (Depending on screen size).
    *   *Labor Profit:* **1,200 - 2,000 EGP per job (Gross Margin: ~80%)**.
2.  **Power Board / Motherboard Component Repair:**
    *   *Average Raw Material Cost:* 50 - 150 EGP (Capacitors, MOSFETs, diodes).
    *   *Retail Repair Charge:* 800 - 1,500 EGP.
    *   *Labor Profit:* **750 - 1,350 EGP per job (Gross Margin: ~90%)**.
3.  **LED/LCD Panel Replacement (Broken Screen):**
    *   *Average Raw Material Cost:* 50% - 60% of new TV retail value.
    *   *Retail Repair Charge:* Panel wholesale cost + 800 - 1,200 EGP labor.
    *   *Labor Profit:* **800 - 1,200 EGP per job (Gross Margin: ~25%)**.

### B. Screen Sourcing & Sales Margins / هوامش أرباح بيع الشاشات
1.  **Refurbished TV Sales (High Margin Loop):**
    *   Purchase damaged-screen or non-working TVs from residents in Hadayek Al-Ahram for **500 - 1,000 EGP**.
    *   Repair them using spare parts from stock (average cost **1,000 EGP**).
    *   Sell the fully refurbished Smart TV with a 6-month shop warranty for **4,500 - 6,000 EGP**.
    *   *Net Profit:* **3,000 - 4,000 EGP per sale (Gross Margin: ~65%)**.
2.  **New Screen Sales (Wholesale-to-Retail):**
    *   Source brand new TVs directly from wholesalers in Giza (Samsung, LG, Tornado).
    *   *Wholesale Margin:* **8% - 15%** markup.

## 5. Revenue Projections & Payback Period / التوقعات المالية وفترة استرداد رأس المال

We project a conservative sales volume starting in Month 2, after the digital portal and new storefront signage are fully operational.

### Monthly Volume Target / المستهدف الشهري:
*   **Average Repairs:** 3 screens per day = 90 repairs/month.
    *   Average repair revenue: 1,800 EGP (Profit margin 70% = 1,260 EGP).
    *   Total monthly repair gross profit: **113,400 EGP**.
*   **Refurbished TV Sales:** 8 units/month.
    *   Average profit per unit: 3,500 EGP.
    *   Total monthly sales gross profit: **28,000 EGP**.
*   **Total Monthly Gross Profit:** **141,400 EGP**.

### Profit & Loss Statement / الأرباح والخسائر:
*   **Monthly Gross Profit:** 141,400 EGP  
*   **(-) Monthly OpEx:** 38,000 EGP  
*   **Net Monthly Cash Flow (Before Taxes):** **103,400 EGP**

### Payback Period / فترة استرداد التكاليف:
$$\text{Payback Period} = \frac{\text{Total CapEx}}{\text{Net Monthly Cash Flow}} = \frac{95,000 \text{ EGP}}{103,400 \text{ EGP/month}} \approx \mathbf{0.92 \text{ Months}}$$

Even with a 30% discount on volume targets, the setup cost will be fully recovered within **1.5 to 2 months**, proving this is a highly feasible, low-risk, and extremely lucrative project.
