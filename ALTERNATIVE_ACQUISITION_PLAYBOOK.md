> **Bypassing LinkedIn Messaging Limits to Win Real Estate & Clinic Clients**

When you hit LinkedIn connection and messaging limits, you must shift from **direct outreach** to other high-impact, limit-free B2B channels.

## 📞 Channel 1: The WhatsApp "Demonstrator" Outbound (أفضل طريقة إغلاق صفقات)
*Since you sell a WhatsApp AI system, the most convincing place to show it is directly on WhatsApp. You can find public WhatsApp numbers of real estate agencies and clinics on Google Maps or their Facebook pages.*

### 🛠️ The Strategy:
1. Find a target real estate agency or clinic.
2. Send a message to their public WhatsApp number.
3. Address their response time and offer a direct live demo.

> احنا طورنا نظام **Real Estate AI Command Center** بالذكاء الاصطناعي الكامل. السيستم ده بيرد على استفسارات العملاء على الواتساب 24 ساعة، بيأهلهم للشراء ويصنفهم كـ VIP Leads، ويسجلهم تلقائياً على لوحة تحكم تابعة للشركة.

## 👥 Channel 2: Facebook Inbound Group Marketing (التسويق الجاذب في المجموعات)
*Facebook groups in Egypt and the Gulf are highly active hubs for business owners and medical directors. Instead of messaging them, you post high-value case studies and let them comment/message you.*

### 📍 Target Groups:
* **Real Estate Egypt:** "عقارات التجمع الخامس", "مطورين عقاريين مصر", "Real Estate Brokers Egypt"
* **Clinics / Healthcare:** "أطباء مصر", "إدارة عيادات ومستشفيات", "Medical Directory Egypt"
