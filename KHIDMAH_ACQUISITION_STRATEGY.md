## 💡 2. The EL NAFEER Solutions for Khidmah

We can pitch three specific systems to solve their operational pain points:

### System A: "Khidmah Home" WhatsApp Command Center (B2C)
* **Problem:** Residents calling call centers to book AC repairs or plumbing is slow and expensive.
* **Solution:** A WhatsApp AI agent that:
  - Asks the user for their location and problem description.
  - Allows them to upload photos/videos of the issue (e.g., a leaking pipe).
  - Integrates with their scheduling system to book a technician slot instantly.
  - Saves the request directly on an executive operations dashboard.

### System B: Smart AI Technician Dispatch Dashboard
* **Problem:** Coordinating hundreds of technicians across Abu Dhabi and Dubai manually leads to massive transit delays.
* **Solution:** An automated dashboard that reads incoming WhatsApp work requests, matches them with the nearest technician based on GPS coordinates and skill certifications (MEP vs Cleaning), and auto-dispatches them.

### System C: Internal HR & Onboarding Bot (for HR Department)
* **Problem:** Onboarding and training thousands of cleaning staff and technicians is an administrative bottleneck.
* **Solution:** A WhatsApp HR Bot for technicians to check their shifts, submit vacation requests, or ask policy questions.
